Is Maximum always better than Auto?

Not necessarily. Emby recommends Auto for most people because it measures available bandwidth. Raising the streaming bitrate can help avoid unnecessary conversion, but may cause buffering on slower links. Maximum also does not guarantee Direct Play.

    COMMON QUESTIONS

    What else should I know?

    Does a higher maximum bitrate force 4K or HDR?

    No. It removes or raises one potential streaming limit. Actual resolution, HDR, codecs and Direct Play depend on the source, player, display, subtitles and server configuration.

    What if my app doesn't have “Maximum”?

    Look for “Max streaming bitrate”, “Video Quality”, “Home network quality”, “Internet quality”, or “Original quality”. Choose the highest numeric bitrate shown; there may be no literal option named “Maximum”.

    Should I change the quality inside a playing video?

    You can, but playback-overlay changes may be temporary. For a persistent preference, first look in the app's main Settings → Playback area. Some clients differ.

    Why is Emby still transcoding?

    Unsupported audio/video formats, subtitle formats, bandwidth limits, device capability and server restrictions can trigger conversion even with the highest bitrate selected.
